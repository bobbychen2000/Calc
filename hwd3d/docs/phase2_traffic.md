## 2. Identity and D1

- **Fields.** `tr.ident = {masked, label, reg, regSrc, callsign, operator, opSrc, icaoType, typeName, typeSrc, category, src}`.
  - The broadcast fields come first: `r`, `t`, `desc`, `flight`, `ownOp`.
  - The relay's inline Mictronics `_mx` fills gaps.
  - The registration can also come from the ICAO address (`nNumberFromHex`, US block only, `regSrc: 'icao_address'`).
  - `typeName` falls back to the ACD model name.
  - `fetchIdent(key)` asks the relay's `/api/ident`. It is never sent for a masked key, and at most once per key per
    10 min.
- **Label.** The callsign if it is an ICAO flight id or differs from the registration, else the registration, else the
  type, else "Aircraft". A key is never used as a label.
- **D1 masking.** A track is masked when any of these holds:
  - the record has `_masked`;
  - `dbFlags & 12` is set (readsb `README-json.md`: PIA = 4, LADD = 8, as P1 quoted it in `docs/phase2_relay.md` §3);
  - the key has the masked form `x` + 8 hex.

  Masking is sticky for the track. A masked track carries type only: `reg`, `callsign` and `operator` are null, and
  `setInfo` also blanks the squawk and the `_mx` registration and operator.
- **Persistence** (`hwdlive.parked.v1`, browser only). Each entry stores the key, ICAO type, category, the masked flag
  and the park pose. No registration or callsign is stored.

## 3. Heights, ground, and the GA phase classifier

**Heights.** Calibration from `refs/cache/adsb/hwd_events.json` [obs]:

```
h = alt_geom − GEOM_OFF − NASR runway surface (ft)
GEOM_OFF = −108.09 ft  (n = 797)
```

- Without `alt_geom`, the classifier uses `alt_baro` minus the running median of (`alt_baro` − `alt_geom`) over
  aircraft that report both. If there is none, it uses `baro_global` (−148.6 ft).
- Per-aircraft offsets are learned as in `derive.py`: 25-ft units only, at least 5 airborne-flag fixes rolling at
  20–50 kt on a runway within 60 s of a ground-flag fix.
- 100-ft transponders are detected as in `derive.alt_quantum` and use `H_CONTACT_COARSE` (80 ft).
- The drawn y is `site.groundY` on the ground. In the air it is the runway-surface height plus h. sfo3d's `GROUND_Y`
  and `GEOID_M` are not used.

**Ground.** The classifier treats a fix as on the ground under the offline rule: the ground flag, or ground by
kinematics (`h ≤ GK_H` 30 ft and `gs < GK_GS` 35 kt). Helicopters use the ground flag only. The drawing applies one
extra condition [inf]: ground by kinematics counts only within 8 m of pavement. Without it, a low, slow approach fix
with a per-unit altitude bias was drawn rolling over the grass of the approach.

**Classifier.** The live classifier ports the offline rules one to one. All 32 constants are copied from
`hwd_events.json` `rules`.
- **Same rules as offline:**
  - the fix states R, L, G, A (H for helicopters), with the 3-point median of h and the motion track;
  - runway episodes and the context scan (`ctx`), including `CTX_GAP`;
  - the episode table (touch-and-go, stop-and-go, full-stop landing, take-off, runway ground move, landing unresolved);
  - hidden transitions, low passes (go-around obs / touch-and-go inf / unresolved);
  - helicopter lift-off, touch-down, hover-taxi and low transit.
- **Evaluation.** It runs causally on a 15-min sliding window of each track's fixes. An event is emitted once its
  context has been decided: the deciding fix has a successor or is older than `HOLE_S`, or `CTX_GAP` has passed.
- **Dedupe.** Events are deduplicated per track. A run that the window start has cut is skipped, because such a run is
  more than 15 min old and was decided long ago.
- **Phase labels.** Labels are written onto the fixes and revised (back-dated) when an episode or low pass is decided.
  Until then a label is `provisional`. The display shows the label of the fix it is at.
- **Phase ids.** All 28 ids of the fork map §3 exist (`PHASES`). The new parts, all [inf]:
  - **Stops:** `parked` after 60 s still, or when first heard still. `hold_short` within 25 m of an `A.holds` bar
    (`hwd_runup_holds.json` `HOLD_R`). `runup` when still in an apron named `*Run-up`/`*Runup`. The `runup`,
    `hold_short` and `line_up` events fire once a stop lasts `MIN_S` (30 s).
  - **Pattern legs** (AIM 4-3-3, recalled, [verify]):
    - The pattern pair is the runway pair on whose side of the midline the aircraft is. South is 10R/28L; north is
      10L/28R (NASR pattern sides, [obs]).
    - The landing direction comes from the HWD runway events of the last 30 min. The default is 28: 92 % of low fixes
      were westbound in the recorded week.
    - A fix is a leg only below TPA + 700 ft and within 4 NM.
    - Legs by track and position:
      - upwind / `initial_climb`: aligned ±30°;
      - downwind: anti-parallel, 0.25–2.5 NM on the pattern side;
      - crosswind: perpendicular, beyond mid-runway, moving toward the pattern side;
      - base: perpendicular, before mid-runway, moving toward the centreline;
      - final: aligned and within 300 m of the centreline.
  - **Scope (D6):**
    - `hwd` on any of: R contact; a G fix on field pavement within 1.5 km; an L corridor fix; a hidden transition; a low
      pass; helicopter ground contact or a hover at ≤ 50 ft within 1.5 km; or two different pattern legs within 10 min.
    - `overflight` at once for emitter categories A3–A5, or after 90 s without that evidence, unless the aircraft is
      closing on HWD below 2,000 ft AGL within 5 NM.
    - An overflight can still become `hwd`.
    - An overflight's airborne phases display as `overflight`.
  - **Tower:** `phaseInfo.tower` is open or closed by local time in `America/Los_Angeles`. The tower is open
    07:00–21:00 (NASR); `phaseLabel` adds "tower closed (CTAF)".

## 4. Motion

- **Display delay.**
  - Replay: `traffic.setLookahead(lookahead_s)`, fixed at 60 s for the P1 packs.
  - Live: the delay adapts to the p90 report age + 0.5 s, between 1.5 and 65 s.
  - A first payload more than 5 s old is history, so it does not set the clock offset.
- **Air:**
  - Interpolation is sfo3d's Hermite. Across a polling hole the reported velocities are kept, because they are the only
    record of the turn flown in the hole. They are not kept if the chord is faster than the aircraft or the two speeds
    differ by more than 2.5×.
  - The body is a critically damped follower:

    | Limit | Value |
    |---|---|
    | horizontal acceleration | ≤ 4.5 m/s² |
    | vertical acceleration | ≤ 2.5 m/s² |
    | vertical speed | 12 m/s (GA), 15 m/s (turboprop), 25 m/s (jets) [inf] |
    | heading rate | ≤ min(20°/s, 4.5 m/s² / speed) |

  - Roll is the coordinated bank `atan(v·ω/g)`, capped at ±35°.
- **Fix round 1 (Phase 2 review) - what changed and why.** The review counted 28 / 27 / 6 visible re-placements per hour
  (an aircraft vanishing for ~0.5 s and reappearing 60-1,295 m away), take-offs that braked to a stop on the runway and
  lifted off at 1-30 kt, ghost-parked aircraft at their old spot, straight chords across grass, and fixed-wing aircraft
  hanging in the air. The root causes were bodies that could not follow their own plans (pivot rule, body lag) and
  fallbacks that ignored the pavement. The motion now comes from `js/live/motion.js` (new, Node-pure):
  - `filletPath(pts, r, ok)`: the taxi route's corners are rounded with 20 m fillets where the wheels stay on pavement
    and the wingtips clear the buildings (halved down to 1 m otherwise) [inf radius].
  - `profilePath(R, D, v0, v1, opt)`: a drivable speed profile over the route - start and end at the fixes' own speeds,
    corner caps `v <= min(sqrt(a_lat * r), w * r)`, the body's own acceleration and braking limits, cruise capped at
    20 kt (25 kt when the hole cannot be taxied at 20); spare time becomes a stop, never a crawl; a profile is feasible
    only if its minimum time is <= 1.15 x the hole. The body follows the profile exactly, so it never has to catch up.
  - `geoHermite(p0, d0, s0, p1, d1, s1, D)`: across an airborne hole a curve shaped by the two reported directions, its
    tangent scale solved so that the curve length equals the hole time x the mean speed, with a flat-topped speed
    profile - no speed spikes, no loops.
  - **Take-off / landing plans** start from the ground fix's own position and speed (a rolling start), route to the
    point where the taxi path joins the runway centreline and line up there; the touchdown is placed >= 60 m before the
    roll-out fix and the hole time is split by the natural roll time; lift-off at VLOF = 0.85 x the FAA ACD approach
    speed, touchdown at 0.95 x, never below 0.7 x (`ga_acd.js` approach speed, [inf] factors). The roll decelerates at
    2.5 m/s2 (3.0 for jets and turboprops).
  - **No chord over grass.** A ground hole uses the taxi route, then the fast-taxi route, then the straight chord only
    if the chord itself stays on pavement, then a forced route if it is at most 1.5 x too slow; otherwise the aircraft
    holds (HWD only, holes > 20 s). A landing roll-out followed by a runway exit is roll-out + taxi.
  - **Debounced ground state.** A slow fixed-wing fix (< 0.45 x its approach speed) is on the ground; one
    airborne-by-altitude fix under 250 ft after a ground fix is held on the ground; a lift-off needs the body at
    >= 0.8 VR, else the target stays on the ground along the nose.
  - **Re-placements** are rare and counted as teleports: thresholds 150 m on the ground, 400 m on a roll, max(250 m,
    8 s x v) in the air, plus a jump of the raw ground target > 10 m in one step. Since fix round 1 a re-placement
    cross-fades with real alpha (the obj shader's screen-door `uAlpha`, `objects.js`), not just a 0.5 s blank.
  - **Live: no hovering.** An airborne track dead-reckoned (straight and level) longer than 20 s fades out; a fixed-wing
    body is never brought to rest in the air.
  - **Ground bodies** follow the target's trail (loop removal for zig-zag trails, catch-up capped at +3 m/s, never above
    25 kt while taxiing); ground bodies more than 3 km from the ARP are not drawn (no pavement model there).
  - **Parking:** a park record is released when the aircraft moves more than 15 m from it while transmitting, or goes
    airborne; on a ground loss the aircraft rests at its last fix pose (or in the hangar), never at an older park pose;
    "parked" is not created for an aircraft first heard standing on a taxiway or taxilane ("stopped on a taxiway").
  - **Helicopters** get still runs and rest poses (skid stance); a ground track whose rest pose lies inside a hangar is
    hidden, not drawn through the walls.
- **Measured (fix round 1).** The reviewer's three windows (heli / pat / night packs) through a harness that follows the
  app's replay path at 10 Hz: visible re-placements 28 -> 5, 27 -> 2, 6 -> 1; lift-offs below 40 kt 12 -> 0 (pat);
  on-ground stops from > 40 kt to < 8 kt within 3 s 14 -> 0; ghost-parked aircraft 0; label contradictions 0. P3's own
  harness `tools/traffic/replay_engine.mjs <window> --strict` now fails on re-placements > 6 / h, ghost parking, slow
  lift-offs, hard stops, hops, hovering and label contradictions (see `docs/phase2_run.md` section 6 for the numbers).
- **Fix round 2 (second review) - what changed and why** (numbers in `docs/phase2_run.md` section 7).
  - **Teleports of any absence.** Fix round 1 counted only absences shorter than 5 s. A fade-out, removal and re-creation
    elsewhere was invisible to both harnesses, and the gate allowed 6 per hour.
    - Now any absence counts. A teleport is split into *engine* (the signal was continuous, fix gaps ≤ 90 s) and
      *signal* (the aircraft was not heard for longer).
    - Both are gated at 0 (signal: HWD traffic).
  - **Silent aircraft stay (`groundLost`).** A ground track whose reports stop used to fade out off an apron and come
    back elsewhere. Now it stays drawn (`tr.stale`), phase `no_signal` "No signal · last position" [inf].
    - It stays at the stop point of its own braking extrapolation (`groundStop`: taxi / roll deceleration, clipped to
      the pavement) for up to 30 min (`LOST_KEEP_MS`). The fix-round-1 text above ("rests at its last fix pose") held
      only on aprons, helipads and hangar-door taxilanes below 1 kt; anywhere else the aircraft faded out.
    - Once heard again it continues from where it is drawn (a continuation anchor), not from the new fix.
    - groundLost waits `MOVING_LOST_MS` + 30 s (`LATENCY_ALLOW_MS`) [inf], so a report that arrives late by its
      `seen_pos` latency no longer races the loss.
    - A heard, moving aircraft that drives into a silent, unparked one fades the silent one (`staleOverrun`).
  - **Far transitions (OAK).** An arrival whose next fix is a ground fix more than 3 km from the ARP flies a curve to a
    touchdown short of that fix and rolls hidden (OAK's runways are not modelled). Before, the generic hole faded
    these arrivals out hundreds of feet up. A departure from there waits hidden at lift-off, then flies the next
    fix's track (`farTransition`).
  - **Ground physics.** A body counts as stationary when its *target* rests (`restPose`), whatever its own speed. The
    seek offset is kept while the target rests and decays from where it is once the target moves. Before, a body
    driving to its seek offset counted as moving, its offset decayed and it shuttled ~12 m to and fro.
    - Ground fixes off the pavement snap to the nearest paved point within 25 m (not inside a building) [inf].
    - Roll fixes snap to the runway axis.
    - Routes get a reversal penalty and lose out-and-back spurs (`despur`).
    - The rest-jitter threshold is 0.3 m.
  - **No straight chord.** The straight air-to-ground fallback ('ag:lin') is gone.
    - A near transition with no feasible plan rolls along the runway axis (touchdown ≥ threshold + 30 m, then the exit
      route; `fallbackTransition`).
    - Flight curves come from `flightCurve`: a Hermite curve unless it needs more than 30° of bank at the flown speed.
      Otherwise a turn-limited Dubins path at 25 / 30 / 35° bank (`motion.js turnFlight`), with a straight final
      (300 m, jets 600 m) and a straight climb-out (500 m, jets 1,500 m) [inf].
    - A contact before the threshold that reports only an altitude stays airborne.
    - Air fixes implying more than 2 g over 2-20 s are rejected (three agreeing fixes override).
    - Harness: `transition_linear` (INFO, now 0) and the gate `misaligned_transitions` (a lift-off or touchdown more
      than 15° off the runway).

## 5. Parking, hangars, physics

- **Spots.** Matching is within 7.5 m (10 m for helicopters) [inf]. It uses the point the spot's `ref_point` stands for:
  main-gear line, GNSS antenna, or planform centre.
  - Fixed-wing aircraft never take helipads.
  - A span above `capacity_span_m` + 0.5 m is still matched when no occupied spot lies within the span. Capacities are
    equal shares sized as if every neighbour were taken.
  - Tie-down tees and rule points give the pose and heading, flipped if the aircraft reported the opposite heading.
    Other spots keep the aircraft's own median pose.
  - Without a reported heading or a headed spot, the nose points to the nearest taxi line [inf].
- **Park events.** `park`/`unpark` events carry the spot id. Zones are the Phase 1 descriptive labels (D3).
- **Hangared** [inf, always]. A ground track loses its signal when it is slow (< 8 kt) and within 8 m of a hangar
  outline (`adsb_signal_edges_8m`). It is then hidden with `park.inside = true`, and a `hangar_in` event fires. Heard
  again in the same place, it emits `hangar_out`.
- **Physics.** Planforms come from the dims records: fuselage, wing trapezoid, h-stab, rotor disc, and tricycle,
  tail-wheel or skid gear.
  - Stationary bodies are placed in turn: headed spots first, then by first seen. Each takes the nearest pose within
    6 m (then 15 m) where every wheel is on pavement, no part is in a building, and the gap to the others is ≥ 1.0 m.
    The 1.0 m is the spot capacity gap that Phase 1 observed at HWD.
  - Moving bodies get a fading offset away from overlaps (sfo3d).
  - Two exemptions: helicopters may stand on unpaved ground, and a Phase 1 door spot ("its nose may enter that hangar")
    waives the building test.
- **Pavement** is `site.paved` (P2) plus `js/aircraft/pave_naip.js`.
  - **Why.** OSM misses real airside asphalt: the wide run-up pads at 28R, apron edges, and taxiway shoulders wider than
    the default ribbons. The drawn ground (NAIP) shows asphalt there, and aircraft are observed standing on it. Before
    the mask, the physics pushed them onto the OSM ribbons, and in the 15-min test 0.3 % of ground frames were
    counted off pavement only because of that gap.
  - **Grey test.** Phase 1's test, with the thresholds re-calibrated [inf]. Saturation is < 0.20 instead of 0.16, and
    excess green < 6.5 instead of 8, because the beige run-up asphalt measures 0.17–0.19.
    - On a 6,000-cell sample, OSM apron cells pass 92.7 %.
    - Aerodrome cells more than 30 m from OSM pavement pass 27.3 %. That sample includes unmapped asphalt and roofs,
      so 27.3 % is an upper bound on grass.
  - **Rules.** The added cells must:
    - lie inside the aerodrome boundary;
    - stay outside car parks, buildings (dilated 1 m) and fences;
    - be connected to OSM pavement and lie within 30 m of it.

    A 5 m closing fills holes left by the paint (hold bars, tees), and one cell of overlap seals the seam with the
    site's 0.5 m grid.
  - **Size.** 154,828 m² added, counting the overlap cells, against 493,718 m² of OSM pavement.
  - **Checks.** Close-ups over NAIP: `out/traffic/mask_runup28R.png`, `mask_west.png`, `mask_hold28R.png`. Some light
    runway shoulders (10L) pass as false positives. That only makes the physics lenient where ADS-B puts an aircraft.
  - **Request to P2:** `site.paved` should include this mask so the renderer and the physics share one pavement model.

## 7. Harness and results

```
python3 -I tools/traffic/ref_window.py out/replay/pack_<w>_meta.json     # offline reference counts + local leak list
node tools/traffic/replay_engine.mjs <w> [--pack PATH --tag _full] [--dump]
python3 -I tools/traffic/plot_tracks.py <w>                                # worst drawn ground tracks over NAIP
node tools/traffic/shoot.mjs --min 33                                      # renders (free port 8763 is checked)
```

**How it runs.** The harness feeds each P1 pack the way the shim does: the first 60 s at once, then each payload at its
time, at 10 Hz with ground physics. It samples every frame for aircraft that are drawn (valid, in the D6 window, alpha
> 0.5).

**The reference.** For each window, the reference is the Phase 1 per-event rows (`hwd_events_local.json`) whose time
falls inside the pack window.

**Runs.** Weekend: Sun 8 Nov 2026 20:00–20:59Z, 138 tracks. Weekday: Mon 9 Nov 21:00–21:59Z, 109 tracks. Both runs
are deterministic: repeated runs gave identical counts. Wall time was 15 s and 8.5 s. `update` took p50 0.19 / 0.12 ms
and p99 2.4 / 1.1 ms per frame in Node.

## 8. What I could not verify, and limits

- **Helicopter dimensions.** No source was read; the values are recalled figures, marked inf. The same applies to the
  DO-260B category meanings, the AIM 4-3-3 leg definitions, and the GA wing / gear / tail family tables.
- **Engine limits.** Every kinematic limit is a design value [inf]: the taxi and roll accelerations, VR, the 2 m/s²
  lateral limit, the touchdown distance, and the 7.5 m spot radius. The harness reports what the drawn motion does
  against them; that is not a measurement of real aircraft.
- **Pattern legs.** The leg thresholds are uncalibrated. Both windows are west flow, so east-flow legs and plans are
  untested.
- **Event context.** Live events depend on contexts of up to 150 s. In replay the display is 60 s behind, so an
  undecided touch-and-go or low pass shows as `provisional` for up to about 90 s (12,754 / 3,898 drawn frames).
- **Live mode.** It was not run against the live relay. Only the replay packs were run. With 6.5–20 s polls the delay
  adapts up to 65 s; this is untested.
- **Ground motion residuals** (§7):
  - Crab above 10°: 1,199 / 659 frames. Path-lateral above 3 m/s²: 1,314 / 819 frames. Both are 2–3 % of the
    42,443 / 31,046 moving path samples.
  - Rare single-frame stop spikes come from the stop-and-pivot rule.
  - 29 / 17 ground ↔ air holes fall back to timed linear paths (no runway alignment found, or the plan did not
    fit the hole).
  - Five aircraft on the weekend still have wheels off pavement in 0.19 % of frames.
- **Not checked or not built:**
  - **App.** The engine was not integrated into P4's `live.html`, and no frame of the real app was rendered with it.
  - **Phone.** The phone renders are of the P3 test page only, not of P4's app (§6).
  - **Tail-wheel types.** The sit angle is not modelled.
  - **Fades.** The obj program has no alpha, so a fade becomes a cut at its midpoint.
  - **Vehicles.** The vehicle path is untested: neither window contains a vehicle within view.

## 9. Deviations from the fork map, and requests

- **`js/aircraft/ga_dims.js`** is hand-written (the rules). The generated data are `js/aircraft/ga_acd.js`.
- **Dims extensions:**
  - `cls: 'jet'` carries `sub`: light, biz or airliner. `turboprop` carries `sub`: single or twin.
  - Extra fields: `engines`, `engType`, `retract`, `tail`, `skidLen`, `category`, `provenance`, `dimSrc`, `clsSrc`.
  - `xNose` is the third wheel's station: the nose wheel, or the tail wheel for tail-draggers.
- **TrafficEvent extensions:**
  - Extra fields: `type` (the offline event name, so counts compare), `runway`, `via`, `evidence`, `x`, `z`, `tower`,
    `place`/`apron` for helicopters.
  - `landing_unresolved` maps to kind `landing`.
  - `low_transit` maps to kind `overflight`.
  - Stops add kinds `runup`, `hold_short` and `line_up`.
- **Traffic API additions:** `setLookahead(s)`, `classifier`, `paved`, `groundY`. `PhaseClassifier` also has
  `prep`, `tick` and `flush`.
- **Dropped:** the direct-polling `Feed` (it would bypass the relay and adsb.lol's 500 requests/day) and `fetchTaf`.
- **New data module:** `js/aircraft/pave_naip.js`. **Request to P2:** fold it into `site.paved` (§5).
- **For P4:**
  - **Setup:** construct `new Traffic({airport, taxigraph, site, parking, persist: mode === 'live'})`. In replay, call
    `traffic.setLookahead(info.lookahead_s)` and use `StreamFeed({history: lookahead_s + 5})`.
  - **Per frame:** `traffic.update` → `physics.resolve(traffic, dt)` → `views.sync(traffic, now, {selectedKey, dt})`.
  - **Labels:** `tr.ident.label` (never the key) and `phaseLabel(tr)`. Show an inferred marker when
    `tr.phaseInfo.src === 'inf'` or `tr.park.src === 'inf'`, and mark `phaseInfo.provisional`.
