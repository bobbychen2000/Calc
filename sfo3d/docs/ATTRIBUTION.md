# Data sources and required attributions

Which third-party data the shipped app and the committed data files contain, under which terms, and the notice each
one requires. Quotes were read on 24 Sep 2026 from the pages named; the local copies kept then under `refs/cache/`
were lost with the container on 2026-11-04 (the URLs are given; CDLA-Permissive-1.0 was re-read on 2026-11-04 from the
SPDX license-list text). Not legal advice; the owner decides.

**Open owner decisions (static close-out, 2026-11-04):** (1) `data/snapshot.js` contains adsb.fi data although adsb.fi's
terms forbid licensing it (row below: licence conflict); (2) the flysfo AODB data in `data/sfo_stands.*` still has no
licence basis and no decision is recorded (flysfo row); since 2026-11-04 it also includes SFO's plan of 4 Nov 2026
(`tools/stands/patch_stands.py`).

>

>
> Measurements on imagery, green paint and extra pavement: USDA NAIP 2024 (USDA Farm Production and Conservation

>
> Runway, lighting and airport data: FAA National Airspace System Resources (NASR), cycle 2026-09-03; FAA Airport

> Stand names, stand allocations and planned aircraft types: San Francisco International Airport (DataSF dataset
> chfu-j7tc, PDDL; flysfo.com flight-status data - see the flysfo row below: no licence basis is published)
