# Traffic audit: the live traffic logic against real recorded traffic at SFO

Research note, 24 Sep 2026 (UTC), written by a research agent for the "Realtime" objective (CLAUDE.md objective 1:
"every arrival, departure, taxiing and parked aircraft is at its real position, and motion is smooth and physically
plausible"). It replays real ADS-B through the app's own code, measures what goes wrong, and designs the fix.
It changes nothing in `js/` or `data/`.

Labels used throughout:
- **Observed**: measured by me from the recording, or seen in a file or response I fetched (URL given).
- **Documented**: quoted from the original source, with the URL.
- **Inferred**: my interpretation.
- **Unverified**: I could not reach or check the original source.

Tools, all new, in `tools/live/`:

What the logic already gets right (observed):
- The runway on final is correct for 6 of 6 arrivals.
- Median cross-track to the landing runway is 1.8–2.9 m; to the parallel it is 227–231 m.
- Interpolated positions are good: airborne within 10 NM, p50 2.8 m and p99 25 m against the truth at the displayed time; taxiing, p50 0.6 m and p99 12 m.
- On-ground heading reproduces the reported true heading (median error 0.05°) except at stands (F7).
- Push-back detection works when the aircraft reports a true heading.

### 1.2 The fix in one paragraph

- **Feed.** Use the relay's 1 Hz merged SSE stream instead of 5 s polls. Render **1.0–1.5 s behind real time**. Measured bounded error: airborne p99 11 m at 1.0 s and 9 m at 1.5 s, with the display 95–142 m behind reality instead of 757 m. Taxiing p99 is 4 m and 3 m.
- **Report hygiene.** Discard the surface `track` field, stale MLAT and non-category vehicle reports.
- **Phase state machine driven by kinematics, not by the transponder flag:**
  - touchdown at the onset of strong deceleration (≥ 1 kt/s twice), found 11–16 s before the flag;
  - liftoff at sustained climb (≥ 192 ft/min twice), found 0.5–2.2 s after the event;
  - the published glide paths for the last mile: 28L 2.85°/TCH 53 ft, 28R 3.00°/TCH 55 ft; the median `alt_geom` of each arrival matches them to within −4 to +15 ft;
  - a take-off requires acceleration.
- **Surface.** Snap taxiing aircraft to the **OpenStreetMap taxiway/taxilane graph**. It fits real tracks with median 0.8 m and p90 4.2 m, against p90 301 m for the current `sfo_details` centerlines, which lack the ramp taxilanes.
- **Parking.** Dock parked aircraft by reported heading plus stand geometry, optionally with SFO's own stand allocation.
- **Reference implementation.** `replay_model.py` implements the state machine. On this recording it gets:
  - every runway right, decided 22–24 km out;
  - every touchdown and liftoff;
  - zero false take-offs;
  - the "last hour" runway statistics equal to the ground truth.

## 2. Data and method

**Recording.** The recorder is `tools/live/record.py`. It polls adsb.fi (`/api/v2/lat/37.6188/lon/-122.3754/dist/40`, key `aircraft`) and adsb.lol (`/v2/point/37.6188/-122.3754/40`, key `ac`) at 1 Hz, and writes `refs/cache/rec/*.jsonl.gz`. Snapshots in the analysed window:
- adsb.fi: 6,372;
- adsb.lol: 3,189.

adsb.lol returned HTTP 429 on 731 of 3,926 requests (19 %); adsb.fi on 6 of 6,379. The `seen_pos` field is documented in readsb `README-json.md`, via [realtime_feeds.md §2](realtime_feeds.md).

**Merging.** `replay_common.build_reports` works per ICAO hex:
- position time = provider `now` − `seen_pos`;
- reports within 0.05 s from both providers are kept once, and missing fields are filled from the other provider;
- positions are converted with the app's own `llToWorld` (`js/geo.js`: ARP 37.6188056 N 122.3754167 W, 110,990 m/deg lat, 111,320·cos φ m/deg lon);
- runway ends are parsed from `js/geo.js RWY_ENDS` (FAA/AirNav, displaced thresholds 28L/28R 300 ft, 1L 640 ft, 1R 560 ft).

Result: 88,028 unique reports.

**Ground truth** (`replay_events.py`). Definitions are in the script header, briefly:
- **Runway**: the end whose true heading is within 20° of the track and whose centreline has the smallest median |cross-track| over the last 4 NM, or over the take-off roll.
- **Touchdown**: estimated as the breakpoint of a two-segment linear fit of groundspeed against time between the threshold and the flag (+5 s), i.e. the onset of strong deceleration. The data are observed; the event is inferred. It is a late bound by about 0–2 s.
- **Liftoff**: estimated as the first of three consecutive vertical rates ≥ 192 ft/min.
- **Exit**: the first report more than 45 m from the centreline.
- **Stop**: gs < 1 kt for ≥ 20 s.
- **Push-back**: motion more than 120° from the reported true heading.
- **Go-around**: aligned below 1000 ft above the runway, descending before, then climbing ≥ 400 ft without a ground report.

**Stand truth.** SFO's own AODB stand allocation (flysfo.com flight-status JSON). It is read through the gate-truth agent's `tools/live/gatecheck.py check --every 120`; see [gate_truth.md](gate_truth.md) for source and terms.

**Weather and runway configuration.**
- KSFO METARs: aviationweather.gov Data API.
- D-ATIS: atis.info mirror (third party; terms unverified). Polled every 10 min by `replay_wx.py`.

**Replay of the app** (`replay_feed.py` + `replay_app.mjs`).
- The payloads are rebuilt exactly as `sfo_live_server.py Hub._merge` produces them: freshest position per hex wins, missing fields are filled, positions older than 60 s are dropped.
- The app polls every 5 s (`relay5`) or every 1 s (`relay1`, SSE-like), or adsb.lol directly every 7 s with provider rotation (`lol7`, as `feed.js`).
- Assumed response arrival: request + 0.35 s, the measured round trip (realtime_feeds §3.2). This is **inferred**.
- The node harness imports `js/live/traffic.js`, `ground.js`, `feed.js` and `airport.js` unmodified.
- `document.createElement('canvas')` is stubbed with @napi-rs/canvas (Skia), so the pavement and building masks are the page's own.
- It runs `traffic.update(now, 0.1 s)` and `physics.resolve()` on a simulated clock, and logs the displayed state every 0.5 s.
- `replay_audit.py` compares each logged row at wall time T with the truth at T − delay.

**Limits.**
- One night-time hour and three quarters.
- The touchdown and liftoff "truths" are estimates from the same ADS-B stream; there is no independent ground truth. The AODB/FlightAware `actual_runway_on/off` could provide one (open question 3).
- Routes: the live API is broken today (F5). For the "with routes" replay I read the same VRS records from the static mirror, with `plausible` assumed true.

### 4.3 Heading on the ground (NEW: the surface `track` is not trustworthy)

- **Availability.**
  - Moving on the ground: `true_heading` 86.8 %, `track` only 4.0 %, none 9.1 %.
  - Still: `true_heading` 51.6 %, none 48.3 %.
  - 99.6 % of `true_heading` values lie on the 360/128° grid (2.8125°; 1090 MHz Riddle, via realtime_feeds §2).
  - While taxiing at 8 kt or more, |true_heading − position-chord direction| is p50 1.8°, p90 6.3°, p99 15°.
- **NEW.** When a surface report carries **both** `track` and `true_heading`, with gs > 5 kt, they disagree by more than 90° in **28.8 %** of adsb.fi reports (736 pairs) and 4.5 % of adsb.lol reports (132). [Clarified by verifier: these figures are for aircraft on the ground at SFO. Over the whole 40 NM feed the rates are 29.4 % for adsb.fi (1,496 pairs) and 17.0 % for adsb.lol (581 pairs).]

  - In 493 adsb.fi surface reports, the `mlat` field lists `track`; in adsb.lol none do.
  - Example (observed): PAL105 on its 28L take-off roll, 07:54:18–07:54:41Z. adsb.fi reported `track 118.0` next to `true_heading 298.12`; adsb.lol reported no `track`.
  - Where this surface `track` comes from is **unverified**. Inferred: MLAT-derived, or computed by the aggregator. [corrected by verifier: now **partly documented**. readsb `README-json.md` (commit 843d8e3, line 90) defines `mlat` as "list of fields derived from MLAT data". ### 4.4 Quantisation

- `alt_baro` and `alt_geom`: 100 % multiples of 25 ft.
- `baro_rate`: 99.1 % multiples of 64 ft/min.
- `true_heading`: 2.8125°.
- `gs`: 0.1 kt on the surface, 0.4–0.5 kt steps airborne (observed values such as 144.5, 144.9).
- `lat`/`lon`: 6 decimals (≈ 0.1 m).

So altitude alone cannot place wheels on a runway better than ±12 ft, which is why the published glide path is used near the ground (§6.4).

### 4.6 The air/ground flag is the 100 kt rule, not touchdown or liftoff (NEW: cause identified)

**Observed:**
- **Arrivals (6/6).** First "ground" report at gs **95–102 kt** (median 98). Last airborne report at 100–107 kt. The flag lags the touchdown estimate by +9.0 to +11.4 s and +523 to +663 m. The last airborne `alt_baro` was 0–50 ft.
- **Departures (13/13).** First airborne report at gs **89–102 kt** (median 94.8). Last ground report at 79–97 kt. Liftoff followed by **13.1–23.6 s (median 20.0)** and **887–1668 m (median 1398)**.

**Documented** — EASA CS-ACNS Issue 4, AMC1 ACNS.D.ELS.020 "On-the-ground status determination" (page 144), <https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/easy_access_rules_for_airborne_communications_navigation_and_surveillance_cs-acns_issue_4.pdf>:
> "For Aircraft with transponders that have access to at least one of the following parameters (ground speed, radio altitude, airspeed) the following validation check should be performed when detected 'on the ground' and the air/ground status should be overridden and changed to 'airborne' if [Ground speed > 50 m/s (100 knots) OR airspeed > 50 m/s (100 knots) OR radio altitude> 15 m (50 feet)]."

AMC1 ACNS.D.ADSB.060 (pages 161–162) adds:
> "validation against the aircraft's typical rotation speed (rather than a fixed value of 50 m/s (100 knots)) might not have been tested in accordance with ETSO-C166b."

FAA AC 20-165B §3.10.2, Note 1 (12/07/15), <https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/advisory_circular/ac_20-165b.pdf>:
> "We recommend that any automatic air-ground determination be more robust than just a simple comparison of ground speed to a single threshold value. Field experience has shown that this method can lead to false air-ground status."

Secondary source (Aerodata AG, Musmann, IFIS 2018), <https://www.icasc.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/IFIS18-0010-Paper.pdf>:
> "Normally the AIR/GND status is determined by WOW switch, but in case ground speed or indicated airspeed exceed 100kts the transponder transits into AIR mode regardless of the WOW switch."

**Inferred:** the transponders here apply that override. On a landing, the IAS or GS test keeps "airborne" until the aircraft decelerates through about 100 kt. On a take-off it declares "airborne" at about 100 kt, long before rotation.

The primary US standard (RTCA DO-260B) is paywalled: **unverified**. Some installations validate against type-specific rotation speeds, so the threshold may differ by aircraft. Re-check on daytime traffic with more types.

This also explains why [realtime_feeds.md §4.5](realtime_feeds.md) saw the first "ground" report about 1.43 km past the threshold on its two arrivals.

**Consequence:** the flag must never be used as the touchdown or liftoff event.

## 6. The improved model (task d)

Everything below is a design (inferred), with its parameters taken from §3–§5. `tools/live/replay_model.py` implements §6.2–6.6 and §6.10 in Python so they can be scored on the recording. Its results are in §6.11.

### 6.1 Architecture

```text
providers (adsb.fi 0.5 Hz v3, adsb.lol 1 Hz) --> relay: merge per hex + hygiene (6.2) --> /api/stream SSE, 1 Hz
      --> client TrackFilter per hex:
            - report buffer (time-stamped, pos_t = now - seen_pos)
            - phase state machine (6.3-6.6), driven by kinematics
            - kinematic renderer at t_display = now - D (6.7):
                  air: interpolation + short dead reckoning, published glide path near the runway
                  ground: map-matched to the taxi graph (6.8), stands (6.9)
      --> GroundPhysics (pavement + clearance, with the mask fixed and the ramps completed)
      --> stats panel (6.10)
```

### 6.2 Report hygiene (relay or client)

1. **Surface heading.** Drop the `track` field when `alt_baro == "ground"`. Heading preference on the ground:
   - `true_heading`;
   - the position chord over ≥ 5 m;
   - the last known heading;
   - the inference in `traffic.js`.

   Evidence: §4.3, F3.
2. **MLAT.**
   - Drop an MLAT position when an ADS-B or ADS-R position for the hex is less than 10 s old.
   - Reject any position that implies more than 1.5 × gs + 50 m of motion since the last accepted one.
   - Drop fields listed in `mlat[]` on the ground.

   Evidence: §4.7.
3. **Vehicles.** Decide once per hex and make it sticky: category C*, `t` SERV/GRND, `adsb_icao_nt`, or `OPS`/`AAST` callsigns with registration `N…QX` (observed pattern; verify). Render vehicles as vehicles, never as aircraft. Evidence: F8.
4. **Non-ICAO.** Keep the `~` in the key, so non-ICAO and ICAO addresses never merge. Evidence: §4.7.
5. **Merge time base.** Position time = `now − seen_pos` per provider (already in the relay). Treat reports within 0.2 s and 5 m as the same report.

### 6.3 Runway assignment (arrivals)

For each runway end R with |track − hdg_R| < 25°, −25 km < a < +1.5 km and not climbing (vertical rate ≤ +300 ft/min):
- accumulate `logL_R += −½(c/σ(a))² − ln σ(a)`, with σ(a) = 25 m + 0.012·|a| (widening with distance);
- the prior is 0.45 for each ATIS approach runway and 0.02 for the others. Parse "ILS RY 28L APP IN USE" with `replay_wx.runway_config`.

Declare FINAL(R) at a posterior ≥ 0.9. Record every later change as a runway-change event; it is allowed until the threshold.

Why the widening σ: SFO has offset procedures (**documented**, FAA d-TPP 2609):
- the QUIET BRIDGE VISUAL RWY 28R chart (Orig 09JUL26) shows track segments of 258°, 278° and 276° (magnetic) via JANYY–RAACL–SHLTN–SAPPP, against the 284° runway course;
- the TIPP TOE VISUAL RWY 28L/R intercepts the localizers "from CHERA 310° heading";
- the RNAV (RNP) Y RWY 28R is an RNP AR approach ("RF required") with curved segments.

These are not all straight-in, so decisions far out must stay soft. Result on this recording: all 6 correct, decided 22–24 km out, with or without the ATIS prior. All were straight-in ILS finals, so the offset procedures are **untested**.

### 6.4 Arrival: final, flare, touchdown, rollout, exit

- **Vertical profile.**
  - Above about 1500 ft AGL: `alt_geom` + N(EGM96) = MSL.
  - Below: blend into the **published path**: 28L 2.85°/TCH 53 ft; 28R 3.00°/55 ft; 19L 3.00°/55 ft; 10L 3.00°/55 ft; others from the d-TPP. It is anchored at the threshold elevation from `RWY_ENDS`, and the blend weight goes 0 → 1 between 1500 and 800 ft.
  - Flare from about 30 ft: exponential decay of height with a 1.5 s time constant, reaching the runway at the predicted touchdown point.
- **Touchdown point, predicted.** GPI + 450 m (inferred from touchdowns at 520–960 m, median about 820 m past the displaced threshold). Corrected online:
  - **deceleration onset**, i.e. two consecutive gs differences ≤ −1.0 kt/s after 100 m past the threshold. Flare deceleration was 0.46–0.78 kt/s and ground deceleration 2.8–4.6 kt/s. In `replay_model.py` this fired 0–6 s before the offline estimate and **11–16 s before the flag** for 6 of 6 arrivals;
  - else the "ground" flag, back-dated by (100 kt − gs_td)/3.5 kt/s;
  - else 2500 m past the threshold and below 150 ft.

  Because the display runs D seconds behind (§6.7), the renderer blends from the predicted point to the detected one over 1–2 s. No bounce, ever: once the state is ROLLOUT, the height is 0 regardless of later "airborne" flags.
- **Rollout.** Spoilers and reversers at touchdown + 1 s. The runway state lasts until |c| > 45 m, which is the runway exit.
  - Observed exits: T, K, B, R.
  - Runway occupancy: 45–80 s for narrow-bodies.
  - Exit speed: 8–38 kt.
- **Label.** "Landed 28L" at the displayed touchdown, not at the flag.

### 6.5 Departure: line-up, roll, rotation, liftoff, climb

- **LINEUP**: on a runway and aligned within 15°, gs < 30 kt.
- **TAKEOFF_ROLL**: requires gs ≥ 50 kt, **or** gs ≥ 20 kt with acceleration ≥ 1.5 kt/s over about 3 s. Observed take-off acceleration from 40 to 90 kt was 4.1–5.8 kt/s (13 departures); the runway back-taxi was 0.1 kt/s. The "airborne" flag (about 95 kt) is only a speed hint.
- **Liftoff**: the vertical rate is ≥ 192 ft/min on 2 consecutive reports, or ≥ 500 ft/min once, or `alt_geom` rises ≥ 50 ft over its median during the roll.
  - In the model this fired 0.5–2.2 s after the offline estimate, and 14–26 s after the flag, for 13 of 13 departures.
  - Rotation is rendered from a type table of liftoff groundspeeds (observed: A21N 157, B39M 173, A359 170, B77W 174, B772 157 kt; extend with more data). Pitch rises at 2.5–3°/s starting at liftoff speed − 10 kt, then climbs at the observed vertical rate. Gear up 3 s after liftoff.
- **Rejected take-off**: gs falls by more than 6 kt within 3 reports after exceeding 40 kt → ROLLOUT.
- **Cold start**: first seen with the "airborne" flag on a runway, aligned, not descending → TAKEOFF_ROLL. Fixes F5 without routes.
- **INITIAL_CLIMB → DEPARTURE**: after 3000 ft or 2 min. Routes are only a label.

### 6.6 Go-around and missed-approach detection

- **Trigger**: in FINAL or FLARE, never touched down, with altitude ≥ lowest + 150 ft **and** vertical rate ≥ +500 ft/min. This produces GO_AROUND(R).
- **Afterwards**: back to APPROACH after 3 min or above 2500 ft; the runway posterior is reset.
- **Display.** Pitch up, gear up at +3 s, a "Go-around 28L" event in the stats panel.
- **Published go-around headings** (TIPP TOE chart, **documented**): 28L "turn left heading 265°"; 28R "heading 280°, climb and maintain 3000".
- **Validation.** No false positive on 6 approaches. **No true positive available** (open question 1).

### 6.7 Latency: play slightly behind real time, with bounded error

`replay_latency.py` gives the error of the displayed position against the truth at the displayed time, and its lag behind reality:

| Feed | Display delay D | Airborne error p50/p90/p99 | Airborne behind reality p50 | Taxi error p99 | Taxi behind reality p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s polls (today) | 7.5 s | 2.7/8.7/16.7 m | **711 m** | 5.5 m | 44 m |
| 1 Hz stream | 0 s (pure dead reckoning) | 3.7/9.6/18.7 m | 3.7 m | 9.4 m | 1.0 m |
| 1 Hz stream | **1.0 s** | 0.7/4.8/11.4 m | 95 m | 4.3 m | 5.9 m |
| 1 Hz stream | **1.5 s** | 0.0/3.1/8.8 m | 142 m | 2.7 m | 8.8 m |
| 1 Hz stream | 3.0 s | 0/0/1.0 m | 286 m | 0.3 m | 17.5 m |

"0" means only interpolation between real reports was needed. The truth is itself interpolated between the same reports, so the interpolation error against reality (the ADS-B accuracy, NACp 9–10 ≤ 30/10 m) is not measurable here.

Design:
- Use the relay's existing `/api/stream` (SSE, one merged update per second).
- Set **D = 1.5 s**, adapted between 1.0 and 3.0 s to the p90 of observed report age.
- Interpolate with Hermite between reports. Past the newest report, dead-reckon with a constant turn rate: horizon ≤ 5 s, then hold.
- Blend corrections over 0.8 s. The error bound is p99 below 10 m in the air and 3 m on the ground.
- For runway events, the kinematic models of §6.4–6.5 cover the detection latency of 1–3 s. With D ≈ 1.5 s, most events are known by the time they are displayed.
- Show "Live · 1.5 s" in the status bar.
- Also switch the relay's adsb.fi poll to the v3 endpoint every 2 s. Its `now` steps in 2 s, so 1 Hz only yields duplicates (realtime_feeds §1).
