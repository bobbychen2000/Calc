### 2.3 Interpretation

- **A-pillar.** Three edges in the drawing are parallel within 1 deg: the windshield's outer edge (33.9 deg above horizontal in side projection), the DV window's front edge (34.6 deg) and the upper front edge of the main pane (34.1 deg). The model uses 45 deg. At WL 2.30 the drawn pillar centre is at x 3.684 (model 3.580), and its side-projected width is 45 mm (model 60). The DV front edge and the main pane's upper front edge are collinear within 9 mm at WL 2.30. The PRO pane, which has no DV, can therefore have one straight front edge on this line, as the photo notes (`refs/photo_notes.md`) describe.
- **Windshield.** The drawn panes are rounded quadrilaterals. The base is arched: x 3.272 at the post and 3.371 at abs(y) 0.5. The aft edge is also arched: x 3.862 at the post and 3.993 at abs(y) 0.5. The outboard edge runs along the 34 deg pillar, from (x 3.473, abs(y) 0.623) back to (x 4.005, abs(y) about 0.50). The model's pane has a straight base at x 3.215, and its 45 deg pillar cuts the outboard-aft half away. The model pane covers only 68 % of the drawn plan area. The centre post is 46 mm wide in the drawing and 68 mm in the model. Seen from ahead, the drawn panes have a nearly level top edge (WL 2.58 to 2.56 out to abs(y) 0.4) and a flat, gently sloping base. The model's panes are arches, because they follow its lower, more rounded roof.
- **Side window.** The PRO-equivalent drawn pane runs from x 3.493 (DV front corner) to 4.356. The model's runs from 3.320 to 4.268. The drawn sill is at WL 2.087 (DV 2.073) against the model's 1.985. The top edge descends aft at 9.2 deg from the pillar corner at WL 2.538, whereas the model's rises 3.5 deg. The drawn aft end is a vertical flat at x 4.356 (WL 2.27-2.40), blended by an R about 0.21 m arc into the sill and an R about 0.12 m arc into the top. The model uses about 0.04 m corners. Measured from the cabin roof, the drawn sill is 0.682 below the crown and the model's 0.645. The drawn top edge at x 4.2 is 0.256 below the crown and the model's 0.159. So the drawn sill is 102 mm higher in absolute terms because the drawn cabin is 139 mm higher (section 3). Relative to the cabin, the model's window sits too high by about 37 mm at the sill and 97 mm at the top edge. The window's fore-aft extent, pillar angle, top-edge slope and aft radii are glazing-definition errors that do not depend on the OML.
- **What the drawing does not show.** It has no dark surround trim (`surround_sdf`); the model's trim stays a styling assumption. The drawing shows the NGX DV window; for the PRO use the union, or the starboard pane mirrored.

## 5. Method and caveats

- Registration accuracy (from `refs/mbp.py`): scale residual below 0.05 mm on the sheet. The x anchor carries +/-21 mm (spinner vs tail anchor). z = ground line; the prop centre comes out 14 mm high. Sheet 2 is about +/-15 mm in x and +/-45 mm in z. Wing and fin section registration is weaker (see the `refs/mbp.py` verifier notes).
- The drawing's glazing loops are enclosed regions of the drawn strokes (stroke centre lines), so they are accurate to about 1-2 mm on the sheet scale.
- The PRO port pane is taken as the union of the NGX side window, DV window and mullion (morphological closing, radius = half the 51 mm mullion gap + 4 mm). The union's concave corners are therefore rounded at about 30 mm near the mullion ends.
- Key-feature edges are straight-line fits over the middle of each edge. Corner radii are circle fits to curvature runs, or to the aft-end arcs.
- Model glazing is evaluated on the current OML. Any change to `model/fuselage.py` moves the panes, so re-run this script after each change.
