    About this video

    An explainer for engineers who are rusty on college math: counting primes, Euler's product, zeta as a sum of spinning arrows, its zeros, the explicit formula that rebuilds the primes from waves, the proved analogue for curves over finite fields, and the idea and construction of the claimed proof.

    Status as of 22 November 2026. Levent Alpöge, working with Claude, released the proof as a Lean library. The Lean and Mathlib communities' joint statement certifies the classical Riemann Hypothesis; Andrew Sutherland's verification page reports the same library also proves it for every Dirichlet L-function. There is no human-readable proof yet, and the Clay prize requires publication, two years and general acceptance. It is a claimed proof.
