# Open questions: exterior

1. **Airframe.** The right-wing registration is modelled as **JA795A**, the airframe confirmed in the new 212-seat
   layout (NH211, 2026). Do you want a specific NH7/NH8 airframe instead?
2. **Yellow ice-detection spot.** ANA reportedly adds one on the black gear-alignment stripe (airlinercafe forum).
   No photo shows its size or position, so it is not modelled.
3. **GE90 logo.** Is there one on ANA's fan cowls? It is faint and illegible in photo 137, so it is not modelled.
4. **Day sky.** Should "day" look like deep-blue high cruise, as the raters recommend, or a hazy low-altitude sky?
   The sky belongs to the lighting area.

## Wave 3

5. **Canoe tail shape.** The raters went back and forth: wedge with a spike, then blunt, then bullet. Both settled on a straight
   keel closing to a small rounded point (_7250). An over-wing photo from the left side (13A-19A) of the 212-seat aircraft would
   settle it.
6. **GE mark.** It is modelled as a ring with the letters GE, low on the inboard fan cowl. Is the real script monogram worth the
   geometry, given that no seat view shows it?
7. **Registration darkness.** Photos range from near-black (KN41) to dark slate (_7250). The model uses near-black navy. Do you
   have a sunlit JA795A close-up?

## Wave 3b

8. **Wing grime level.** The skin now carries a photo-derived grime layer (from the JA795A flap, IMG_7250) at about 2.6 %, plus
   hinge and TE darkening. The photos range from almost clean (KN46) to streaky (7250). Should it look like a recently washed or a
   working airframe?
9. **Spinner colour.** It is now mid grey with the white swirl (KN Aviation 7A close-up). An earlier note said black (JA796A).
   Which matches the airframe you want shown?

## Wave 3c

10. **GE90 fan face.** Both w13 raters ask for a straight-on photo into a JA795A (or any ANA 77W) GE90 inlet, to check the blade
    ring and liner tone. From the 11A / 11K seats the lip hides most of the blades, so the current look (#4a4f55 liner graded to
    dark at the fan, three joint rings, grey blades with some sheen) follows LALF _136 and knav 7A _9 only.
11. **Canoe shading is tuned for the day sun.** The exterior pass has no shadow map, so the canoe and nacelle bakes include a
    term for the day sun direction (az -60). At sunset the sun comes from the other side and is weak, so the effect is small,
    but say if you want the day look to change the sun position.
