# Questions for the user (monuments)

Nothing is blocked on these. Each item says what the model assumes for now.

1. **Door-2 galley passage orientation.** The LALF photos (`lalf_133`/`134`) show the monitor galley across the passage from
   an ash wall with framed flaps. The model assumes the camera looks toward L2, which puts the monitor galley forward of the
   passage, facing aft. If the photos were taken toward R2, the two sides swap. Do you have a photo that shows which side it is?
2. **Door-2 forward lav doors.** The model has them opening onto the door-2 cross-aisle, which is the usual 777 arrangement.
   Rater A reads the TPG row-5 photo as showing the doors on the rows 5-6 aisle side. Which is right?
3. **Jump seats at doors 3 and 4.** No photo found. Every free face there is a lav door or a counter, so the model has no jump
   seats at those doors.
4. **Door-3 bar in service.** Only ANA's 2019 render was found. OMAAT calls it a "small snack bar". Do you have a real photo?
5. **Door-5 rear galley and lavs.** No photo of the new layout was found. The model follows the ANA seat map: two fore-aft
   galleys, with a lav outboard of each.
6. **Door-3 monument depth.** The map suggests lavs about 1.5 m deep; the model uses 1.05 m. Deepening them moves rows 17-27
   about 0.5 m aft. Should the map win?
7. **R-side door linings (wave 3).** The ANA L2 photos (lalf_133/134) show the viewing window forward of the handle housing
   and the arming window upper aft. The model uses the same fore-aft layout on both sides. Do you have an R-side ANA 777 door
   photo to confirm it?
8. **Door-3 bar and door-5 galley in service (wave 3).** Raters searched again (TPG, LALF, KN Aviation, Sam Chui, OMAAT) and
   found only ANA's 2019 bar render. Is the bar's upper-left section a roller blind or a backlit washi panel in service?
9. **Welcome-galley cabinet glass (wave 3b).** The engine has no blended glass for monuments, so the smoked glass door is
   drawn with no pane: its lit contents are toned about 0.85x, with one reflection line. omaat_room_32 and lalf_134 show
   the contents clearly through a light tint. A darker tint would need a transparent material from the lighting side.
10. **Door-4 / door-5 galleys (wave 3b).** No photo exists of the new rear galley. The inserts there now follow the real
    ANA snack-bar galley (sans_38 / sansY_44): satin inserts, ovens with yellow-green label windows, paired brewers and
    copper turn-buttons. A photo of the door-5 galley would confirm the layout.
11. **Welcome-galley cabinet glass (wave 3d).** Opaque reflection strips on the pane read as wires or sticks inside the
    cabinet (w17a), so the pane again has no glass cue. A real smoked-glass look needs a blended or transparent material
    for monuments, which belongs to the lighting side. For now the pane stays open, with the contents toned down.
12. **Door-2 welcome galley (polish F).** The OMAAT photo the welcome galley was built from (omaat_j32) turns out to be the
    door-3 bar (OMAAT: "between the middle and rear business class cabins"), so the bar now follows it and shares the
    welcome galley's face. No in-service photo has been found that is shown to be the door-2 galley; LALF's 133 / 134 are
    captioned only as "a galley divides the two business class cabins", which also fits door 3. The model keeps the
    door-2 galley as it was (same face as the bar). Do you have a photo of the door-2 galley?
13. **Bar left bay: blind up or down (polish F).** The 2019 press photo (Traffic News) has a black blind pulled down over
    the left bay; OMAAT 2020 and KN Aviation show it raised with the ovens visible. The model shows it raised, rolled
    into a black head. Raters also note the 2019 left bay is wider than the 2020 oven column; the model follows 2020.
