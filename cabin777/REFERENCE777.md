# ANA 777-300ER "THE Suite / THE Room" — reference sheet (SFO–NRT, NH7/NH8)

Status legend: **[V]** verified in a written source · **[D]** derived by arithmetic from verified data ·
**[A]** approximation (no written source found; standard-777 or engineering estimate) · **[C]** sources conflict.

## Route / aircraft
- NH7/8 SFO–NRT alternates new and old 777-300ER configurations (OMAAT guide, updated 2026-06-03). [V]

## Airframe
- Fuselage outer diameter 6.20 m, circular (MHI, Jenkinson, Wikipedia). [V]
- Cabin width 5.87 m / 231 in (Aircraft Interiors Intl, Wikipedia 777X); 5.86 m (aircraftinvestigation). [V]
- Windows 15 × 10 in (380 × 250 mm), 15 in vertical (Kawasaki: 390 mm height). [V]
- Window pane centre 1.02 m above the cabin floor (glass 0.82-1.23 m): Boeing D6-58329-2 (777-200LR/-300ER ACAPS) fig. 2.5.1 cross-section, measured on the vector drawing (scale checked against the 229 in cabin width and the 6.20 m skin); side view fig. 2.2.2 gives 0.97-0.98 m above the door sill. Outboard bin lip 1.58 m. [V]
- Type A door lining: forward hinge; viewing window a tall slot (~0.14 x 0.32 m glass) at the window belt, 0.28 m from the door centre (aft in the lining video, forward in LALF's L2 photos lalf_133 / 134; the model follows LALF, lining mirrored fore-aft [C]); handle housing with red arc across the upper middle; arming-indicator box in the upper forward corner; full-width slide bustle to ~0.75 m (ANA 777 lining photos, TPG ANA 777-300ER door 2L). [V]
- Door cross-aisles: grey vinyl between the galley blocks, warm-brown carpet only in the door bays (sans_09, sp_py09, TPG door 2L). Door-3 ceiling is a flat dark bronze soffit with downlights (b_lalf_c133 / c134) [V]; ceilings at doors 1, 2, 4, 5 unphotographed [A].
- Raked tip ends in a point (~0.20 m tip chord) with the leading edge sweeping up into it (KN41, alv_7331) [V shape, A chord/upsweep]. GE monogram low on the inboard fan cowl, grey spinner (ANA window videos, KN Aviation 7A) [V]. JA795A registration on the wing upper surface, near-black navy (The Alviator, seat 26K) [V].
- Frame pitch 21 in / 533 mm: 777-300 plugs 5.3 m = 10 frames, 4.8 m = 9 frames (Flight); FAA AD STA 825+210 / 1434+189. [D]
- One window per frame bay → window pitch 533 mm; ~89 windows per side (aircraftinvestigation) is consistent. [D]
- Doors: 5 per side, translating Type A, 42 × 74 in (1.07 × 1.88 m) (Boeing ACAP text). [V]
- Door 3 is the overwing door added in Section 44; slide stowed in the aft wing-body fairing, deploys aft over the wing (Flight). [V]
- Floor "somewhat below the geometric centre" (Boeing patent 6,152,400). Model: circle centre 0.92 m above floor. [A]
- Bins: Signature Interior, outboard + centre bins; outboard large bin ≈ 218 cm long (4 frames), small ≈ 106 cm (2 frames); centre bin 108 cm long, 162 cm across, 55 cm tall (resale listings of removed 777 bins). [V]
- 6 ft 3 in (1.9 m) person stands beneath closed bins (Boeing 2002). Curved ceiling, indirect lighting. [V]
- All-LED lighting on 777 since 2012 (Boeing/DOE). [V]
- Wing: span 64.8 m incl. raked tips (+2 m each), sweep 31.6° at quarter chord, area 436.8 m². [V]
- GE90-115B: fan 3.25 m, nacelle max Ø ≈ 4.2 m (Wikipedia GE90), spanwise ≈ 9.9 m from centreline (Jenkinson 0.326 b/2). [V]/[D]

## Monuments (ANA seat map, TPG, OMAAT, Business Traveller) [V] unless marked
- Front: galley; lavatory with bidet in front of seat 1A. First class has two lavs (OMAAT).
- Between rows 2 and 5: bulkhead + curtain (FF).
- Door 2: two lavs between rows 6 and 7 (behind 6C/6H), galley + welcome board at L2.
- Door 3: two lavs with bidets between rows 16 and 17; galley that becomes a self-service bar (roller blinds, backlit washi-style panels).
- Door 4: galleys in front of row 30 (centre, where row 30 has no D–G seats [D]); wheelchair lav in front of 30A–C; 27A/C beside a lav.
- Door 5: galley at the back; lavs on both sides at the back (three in total, The Alviator); diaper-table lav behind 42F/G.
- Door-3 bar in service: OMAAT's omaat_j32 is the bar ("between the middle and rear business class cabins", NH109 Jan 2020): warm-charcoal carcass, control panel over two ovens, monitor over two open bays, tall glass-door cabinet, near-black counter (also knav_31). [V]
- Bar left bay: blind down in the 2019 press photo (wider bay), raised with ovens showing in 2020 (OMAAT, KN Aviation). Model follows 2020. [C]
- LALF 133 / 134 are captioned only "a galley divides the two business class cabins" (door 2 or 3); no photo is proven to show the door-2 welcome galley. [C]
- ANA 777 galley inserts: satin aluminium, ovens with yellow-green label windows, paired brewers, copper turn-buttons (sans_38, sansY_44) [V]; the new door-4/5 galleys themselves are unphotographed [A].
- Shared monitors in front of row 25 and in front of row 30 / 31D–G. Bassinets 16D/H, 20D/H, 25A/E/F/K, 31E/F.

## THE Suite (First) — JAMCO; design Acumen, supervision Kengo Kuma [V]
- 1-2-1 A | D G | K, rows 1–2, movable full-height partition between D/G. Open-topped, doors do not reach ceiling. [V]
- 43 in 4K Panasonic screen filling the front wall; ottoman below the full suite width. [V]
- Seat not centred: narrow console shelf full length by the window, slim wardrobe on the aisle side (closet in the thick door). [V]
- Width ≈ 33–34 in seat, suite up to 38 in, bed ≈ 76–81 in. Wall height [C] ("not that high" vs "can't see anyone unless standing"). Model 1.30 m. [A]
- Colours: official dark wood; in person grey-brown/bronze shell, wood-grain panels + grey door, grey seat, blue cushion accent. [V]
- Photos f_17300-17314 [V]: real photos show warm taupe-grey shells, dark straight-grain wood caps, warm mid-grey tweed seat
  and ottoman, violet-navy pillow; renders show vertically fluted exterior shells with an LED line at the floor, a long window
  console with keypad + touch remote, a lit reading spot + vertical light strip on the screen-wall pier, flat seat back with a
  slate flap headrest, wardrobe with coat hook and lit literature niche.
- Electric two-stage shades (sheer + blackout), buttons under each window. [V]
- Caps a warm greige, darker than the walls (lit cap / wall ≈ 0.74, f_17313); aisle flanks espresso straight-grain wood (omaat_f60). [V]

## THE Room (Business) — heavily customised Safran "Fusio"; Acumen + Kengo Kuma [V]
- Odd rows rear-facing (A E F K) further from the aisle, even rows forward-facing (C D G H) closer to the aisle. [V]
- Seats are wedge-shaped; foot sections of opposite-facing seats sit side by side; your side table sits over the neighbour's footwell. [V]
- 107 in per nested pair; 35–38 in wide; bed 71–72 in; footwell 21 in at mouth → 12 in, 14 in high. [V]
- Two-part door: sliding panel from the monitor monument + pop-up panel from the armrest. [V]
- 24 in 4K touchscreen; bi-fold rosewood-grain table under the monitor; cabinet with swivel mirror. [V]
- Photos c_27300-27316 [V]: thin flat cushion + flat back (one seam) on a dark base; slate Ultraleather headrest flap;
  navy pillow; teal belt; charcoal shells with lighter cap rails; pale grey-beige ash (fine straight grain) on aisle-facing
  monument ends, door leaves, cabinets and the centreline wall's upper band; round reading spots in the shell corners;
  red life-vest tabs; seat controls in a dark rounded pod; seat-number plaques on the aisle caps.
- Colours: light wood-tone exterior (light Japanese ash), dark-grey/charcoal seat textiles (in person light grey [C]),
  Ultraleather on armrests/headrests, deep-blue accent inside compartments, dark slate stone accent. [V]
- Electric two-stage shades; individual air nozzles; centre bins present. [V]
- Boarding bundle at every seat: white pad + blue duvet in clear film, blue pillow on it, white pillow behind; bed comforter
  matte blue, tone-on-tone diagonal check ~1.4 cm (tpg_31/42/46/76/81, kn_17, omaat_room_15). [V]

## Economy — Recaro [V]

## Lighting / white balance (official photos)

  (Sam Chui −4..+2, OMAAT −12..−32). [V] The model follows each cabin's official photos (lighting Q17). [A]
