## Airframe
- Fuselage outer diameter 6.20 m, circular (MHI, Jenkinson, Wikipedia). [V]
- Cabin width 5.87 m / 231 in (Aircraft Interiors Intl, Wikipedia 777X); 5.86 m (aircraftinvestigation). [V]
- Windows 15 × 10 in (380 × 250 mm), 15 in vertical (Kawasaki: 390 mm height). [V]
- Frame pitch 21 in / 533 mm: 777-300 plugs 5.3 m = 10 frames, 4.8 m = 9 frames (Flight); FAA AD STA 825+210 / 1434+189. [D]
- One window per frame bay → window pitch 533 mm; ~89 windows per side (aircraftinvestigation) is consistent. [D]
- Doors: 5 per side, translating Type A, 42 × 74 in (1.07 × 1.88 m) (Boeing ACAP text). [V]
- Door 3 is the overwing door added in Section 44; slide stowed in the aft wing-body fairing, deploys aft over the wing (Flight). [V]
- Floor "somewhat below the geometric centre" (Boeing patent 6,152,400). Model: circle centre 0.92 m above floor. [A]
- Bins: Signature Interior, outboard + centre bins; outboard large bin ≈ 218 cm long (4 frames), small ≈ 106 cm (2 frames); centre bin 108 cm long, 162 cm across, 55 cm tall (resale listings of removed 777 bins). [V]
- 6 ft 3 in (1.9 m) person stands beneath closed bins (Boeing 2002). Curved ceiling, indirect lighting. [V]
- All-LED lighting on 777 since 2012 (Boeing/DOE). [V]
- Wing: span 64.8 m incl. raked tips (+2 m each), sweep 31.6° at quarter chord, area 436.8 m². [V]
- GE90-115B: fan 3.25 m, nacelle max Ø ≈ 4.2 m (Wikipedia GE90), spanwise ≈ 9.9 m from centreline (Jenkinson 0.326 b/2). [V]/[D]
